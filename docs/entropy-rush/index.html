Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Last updated: 13 November 2026
The short version
  • You don't create an account, and we don't ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress (coins, levels, stars, skins and settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show, measure and protect ads, as described below.
  • You can limit personalised ads in your phone's settings and, where applicable, in the game's privacy options (🔒).

1. Who we are

Entropy Rush ("the Game") is published by Vikaranet (Pty) Ltd, a private company registered in Botswana (registration no. BW00010041782), Gaborone, Botswana ("Vikaranet", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information is processed when you play the Game, why, and the choices you have.

Contact (privacy, data requests and support): support@vikaranet.com

2. Information the Game stores on your device

To save your progress, the Game keeps the following on your device: coin balance, unlocked levels and stars, high score, owned skins and upgrades, missions, daily-reward and chest timers, and settings such as sound on/off.

  • We do not receive this information. It is not sent to Vikaranet's servers; the Game has no servers of its own.
  • If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this game data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings.
  • Uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage deletes this data from your device.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is funded by ads served by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. This includes banner ads, full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards.

To deliver ads, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the 🔒 privacy button on the Game's home screen. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Your ad choices on Android

  • To reset or delete your advertising ID, or to opt out of personalised ads, open your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • You can also manage Google ad settings at My Ad Center.

4. Fonts and network access

When online, the Game may load its display font from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this sends your device's IP address to Google so the font can be delivered. See Google Fonts privacy. If you play offline, the Game uses your device's built-in font instead.

6. In-game currency and purchases

Coins ("J") in the Game are virtual and have no real-world value. The Game has no in-app purchases. Rewards from ads are granted inside the Game only.

7. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the Game, email support@vikaranet.com and we will help, including asking Google to delete ad-related data where possible.

8. Legal bases and international transfers

Where data protection laws such as Botswana's data protection law or the EU/UK GDPR apply, the ad processing described above relies on your consent where consent is required, and otherwise on legitimate interests in funding a free game and preventing ad fraud. Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards.

9. Retention

We do not store personal information on our own systems. Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Google keeps ad-related data according to its own retention policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we don't hold personal data about you, most requests are best handled through your device settings and Google's tools linked above. You can also email support@vikaranet.com, and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Security

The Game serves its content from within the app over a secure connection, does not ask for sensitive permissions, and does not transmit your game progress anywhere. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we design the Game to keep as little data as possible.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the Game adds new features or partners. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
