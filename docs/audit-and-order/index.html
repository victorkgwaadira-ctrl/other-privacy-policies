Privacy Policy · Effective 11 November 2026
In short: Audit & Order has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID and similar technical data to serve and measure those ads.

1. Who we are

Audit & Order (the "App") is published by Vikaranet (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in Botswana (registration no. BW00010041782), Gaborone, Botswana ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the App's handling of your information.

Contact: support@vikaranet.com

2. Information the App stores on your device

The App saves your game progress on your own device: unlocked cases, best grades, assets recovered and your rank. This data is never sent to us, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

3. Information collected for advertising

The App is free and supported by ads. These include optional "rewarded" ads that you choose to watch for an in-game bonus, and occasional full-screen ads between cases. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the App may collect:

DataPurpose
Device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)Advertising, ad measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention
IP address, used to estimate your general (country or city level) locationServing relevant and legally compliant ads, fraud prevention
Device and app information: model, OS version, language, app version, crash and performance dataDelivering ads correctly, analytics, diagnostics
Ad interactions: ads viewed and tappedAd measurement and rewarding you for watched ads

Google handles this data under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your advertising ID or IP address. We only see aggregated reports, such as the number of ad impressions.

All of this data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices and consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the App shows a consent message from Google before ads use your personal data. You can accept or decline personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy choices button on the App's title screen.
  • Personalised ads: on Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You can play every case without ever watching one.
  • Delete your data: clear the App's storage or uninstall the App to remove your saved progress. To ask us anything about your data, email us at the address above.

5. Permissions

The App requests only: internet access (to load ads and fonts), network state (to check connectivity for ads), vibration (game feedback), and the advertising ID permission used by AdMob. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, files, precise location or phone calls.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and you want their data removed, contact us and we will help.

7. Data retention and security

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Advertising data is kept by Google according to its retention policies. We hold no personal data on our own servers for this App.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws may give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, or to object to it. These laws include Botswana's data protection law and, where it applies, the EU/UK GDPR. Because we do not hold personal data about you for this App, most requests are best handled through the controls in section 4 or directly with Google. You can still contact us, and we will respond within 30 days.
