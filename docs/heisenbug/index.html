Heisenbug: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · App: Heisenbug: Quantum Puzzle (Android, package com.vikaranet.heisenbug)
In short: Heisenbug has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information, such as the advertising ID, to show and measure those ads.

2. Information the app keeps on your device

The app saves your unlocked levels, stars and score in the app's local storage on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

3. Information collected for advertising

We use Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial (full-screen) and rewarded video ads. When ads are requested and shown, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city)
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed, clicked or completed, and crash or performance data from the ads SDK

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, personalise ads. Vikaranet does not receive your advertising ID or IP address. We only see summary reports from AdMob, such as total ad views and earnings.

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message the first time you open it. You can choose whether Google may use your data for personalised ads. If you say no, you may still see non-personalised ads. These are based on context, such as the app itself, rather than your profile.

5. Your choices

  • Limit ad personalisation: on most Android phones, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID. Ads will then not be personalised.
  • Delete your game data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Ask us a question or make a privacy request by emailing privacy@vikaranet.com. We will reply within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to its use, or to withdraw consent. This includes rights under Botswana's data protection law and the EU/UK GDPR. We hold no personal data about you on our own systems. For data processed by Google, you can use Google's own tools at myaccount.google.com, or email privacy@vikaranet.com and we will help where we can.

7. Children

Heisenbug is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, email privacy@vikaranet.com and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by the AdMob SDK as described above.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files, photos or precise location.

9. Data retention and security

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies. The app sends nothing to Vikaranet's servers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add new features or ad partners. The effective date at the top will change, and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
