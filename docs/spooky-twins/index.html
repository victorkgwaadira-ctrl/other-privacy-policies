Spooky Twins

Privacy Policy · Effective 13 November 2026
In short: Spooky Twins has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. Your levels, stars, points, hints and settings stay on your phone. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID and similar technical data to serve and measure those ads.

1. Who we are

Spooky Twins: Entangled Puzzle (the "App") is published by Vikaranet (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in Botswana (registration no. BW00010041782), Gaborone, Botswana ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the App's handling of your information.

Privacy and data requests: privacy@vikaranet.com
General support: support@vikaranet.com

2. Information the App stores on your device

The App saves the following on your own device: completed levels and stars, points, Bonus Vault progress, remaining hints, and settings such as sound on/off and 3D or flat view. This data is never sent to us, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

In 3D view, the App reads your phone's tilt (motion sensor) to move the camera. This is used only on your device at that moment. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

3. Information collected for advertising

The App is free and supported by ads. These include a banner at the bottom of the screen, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional "rewarded" videos you choose to watch for extra hints, extra time or double points. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the App may collect:

DataPurpose
Device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)Advertising, ad measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention
IP address, used to estimate your general (country or city level) locationServing relevant and legally compliant ads, fraud prevention
Device and app information: model, OS version, language, app version, crash and performance dataDelivering ads correctly, analytics, diagnostics
Ad interactions: ads viewed and tappedAd measurement and rewarding you for watched videos

Google handles this data under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your advertising ID or IP address. We only see aggregated reports, such as the number of ad impressions.

The App also loads its display font from Google Fonts. This request contains your IP address and standard device information but no game data. All of this data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices and consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the App shows a consent message from Google before ads use your personal data. You can accept or decline personalised ads. To change your choice later, clear the App's data (Settings → Apps → Spooky Twins → Storage → Clear data) and the message will appear again. Clearing data also resets your game progress.
  • Personalised ads: on Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Rewarded videos are optional. You can play every level without ever watching one.
  • Delete your data: clear the App's storage or uninstall the App to remove your saved progress. To ask us anything about your data, email privacy@vikaranet.com.

5. Permissions

The App requests only: internet access (to load ads and fonts), network state (to check connectivity for ads), and the advertising ID permission used by AdMob. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, files, precise location or phone calls. The motion sensor used for 3D tilt needs no permission and is described in section 2.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and you want their data removed, email privacy@vikaranet.com and we will help.

7. Data retention and security

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Advertising data is kept by Google according to its retention policies. We hold no personal data on our own servers for this App.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws may give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, or to object to it. These laws include Botswana's data protection law and, where it applies, the EU/UK GDPR. Because we do not hold personal data about you for this App, most requests are best handled through the controls in section 4 or directly with Google. You can still email privacy@vikaranet.com, and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
